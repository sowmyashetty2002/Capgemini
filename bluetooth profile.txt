service access points in bluetooth

1. Hands-Free Profile (HFP): Allows a Bluetooth device to be used as a hands-free headset for a mobile phone.

2. Advanced Audio Distribution Profile (A2DP): Used to stream audio from one device to another over a Bluetooth connection, such as music from a smartphone to a wireless speaker.

3. Audio/Video Remote Control Profile (AVRCP): Allows a device to control the playback of audio or video on another device, such as a TV remote.

4. Human Interface Device Profile (HID): This profile allows a Bluetooth device to act as a mouse or keyboard, allowing control of a computer from a distance.

5. Object Push Profile (OPP): Allows two devices to exchange data such as pictures, contacts, and calendar events.

6. Personal Area Network Profile (PAN): This profile allows two devices to create an ad-hoc network, allowing them to share an Internet connection.
